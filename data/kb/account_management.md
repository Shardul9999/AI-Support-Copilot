# Account Management

## Account Deletion

Customers who wish to permanently delete their Acme account should email privacy@acme.in from the email address associated with their account. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days, in line with applicable data protection requirements. Once processed, order history, saved addresses, and payment methods are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Customers with a pending order should note that account deletion does not cancel or refund that order — the order will still be fulfilled and support for that specific order remains available by referencing the order number directly, even after the account itself is deleted.

## Password Resets

Password resets are self-service: the customer clicks "Forgot Password" on the login page and receives a reset link by email, valid for 1 hour. Support cannot manually reset a password or view a customer's current password under any circumstances — this is a hard security boundary, not a convenience limitation.

## Updating Contact Information

Customers can update their email address, phone number, or saved addresses directly from the "Account Settings" page while logged in. If a customer no longer has access to their account (e.g., they lost access to their registered email), support can update contact details manually after verifying identity via the phone number on file plus the customer's most recent order number.
