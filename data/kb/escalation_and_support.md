# When to Escalate to a Human Agent

Support tickets should be escalated to a human agent rather than auto-resolved whenever any of the following apply:

- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human or a manager.
- The question involves a legal, regulatory, or safety complaint (e.g., a product injury claim).
- The order value is above Rs 20,000, regardless of how routine the question appears.
- The customer's account is flagged VIP or Enterprise in the CRM.
- The answer would require information not present in the knowledge base, rather than a paraphrase of something that is present.

Tickets that do not meet any of the above criteria, and where the knowledge base directly and unambiguously answers the question, may be resolved automatically.

## Response Tone

All responses, automated or human, should be polite, concise, and avoid over-promising specific dates or outcomes the company cannot guarantee (e.g., never promise same-day resolution for a courier-side delay, since Acme does not control the courier's timeline).
