# Payments and Billing

Acme accepts UPI, major debit and credit cards, and net banking at checkout. Cash on delivery is available for orders under Rs 5,000, subject to serviceability at the delivery pincode.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails but the amount is debited from the customer's bank account, the amount is automatically reversed by the payment gateway within 5-7 business days. Acme does not hold or manually process these reversals — they happen automatically at the bank/gateway level. If the reversal has not appeared after 7 business days, support should ask for the payment reference ID (shown in the customer's bank statement) to escalate directly with the payment gateway.

## Invoices

A GST-compliant invoice is generated automatically for every order and emailed to the customer's registered email address within 24 hours of dispatch. If an invoice is needed for a business/GST claim and has not arrived, it can be regenerated and resent from the order details page, or by support on request.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer reports being charged twice for the same order, support should first check whether two separate orders were actually placed (a common cause) before treating it as a duplicate-charge error. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days once verified.
