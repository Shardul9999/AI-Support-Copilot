# Refund Policy

Acme offers a full refund, no questions asked, for any purchase returned within 14 days of the original purchase date. The 14-day window is calculated from the date the order was delivered, not the date it was placed, since delivery times vary by region.

To request a refund within the 14-day window, the customer should reply to their order confirmation email with the words "refund request" and their order number. Refunds are processed to the original payment method and typically appear on the customer's bank or card statement within 5-7 business days after approval.

After the 14-day window has closed, Acme no longer issues cash refunds. Instead, returns made between day 15 and day 30 are eligible for store credit equal to the purchase price. Store credit is issued as a code that can be applied at checkout on a future order and does not expire.

No refunds, cash or store credit, are issued after 30 days from delivery, except in cases covered by the Damaged or Defective Items policy below.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or is found to be defective, the 14-day and 30-day windows above do not apply. The customer should contact support with photos of the damage within 60 days of delivery, and Acme will issue a full refund or a free replacement, whichever the customer prefers, regardless of how much time has passed since delivery.

## Sale and Clearance Items

Items purchased during a clearance sale (marked "Final Sale" on the product page) are not eligible for cash refunds under any circumstances, but remain eligible for store credit within the standard 14-day window.
