# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping within India takes 3-5 business days from the date the order is dispatched, not the date it is placed. Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours of being placed, except during sale events, when dispatch can take up to 3 business days due to volume.

Shipping is free on all orders above Rs 999. Orders below that amount are charged a flat Rs 79 shipping fee, regardless of order weight or destination.

Acme currently ships only within India. International shipping, including to neighboring countries such as Nepal, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka, is not supported at this time. There is no workaround or forwarding service offered by Acme for international customers.

## Tracking

Once an order is dispatched, the customer receives a tracking link by email and SMS. The tracking link updates within 24 hours of dispatch and shows real-time courier status. If a tracking link shows no movement for more than 3 business days, the customer should contact support so the shipment can be traced with the courier partner directly.

## Delayed or Lost Packages

If a package has not arrived within 10 business days of dispatch (i.e., well past the standard 3-5 day window), it is considered delayed. Support should first check the tracking link for courier-reported delivery attempts before escalating. If a package is confirmed lost by the courier, Acme ships a free replacement immediately; the customer does not need to wait for a refund process and does not need to return the lost item, since it never arrived.
